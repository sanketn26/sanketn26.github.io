# About

I’m Sanket.

I’ve spent most of my career building software, distributed systems, data
platforms and infrastructure.

Over the years, I’ve set aside many ideas I wanted to explore. Before stepping
into another corporate role — and the commitments and constraints that come with
it — I’m giving myself the time to pursue them properly and turn them into
open-source software.

I’m particularly interested in distributed systems, data engineering,
observability, developer tools and AI systems.

This site is a record of that journey.
